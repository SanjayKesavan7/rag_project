## Classic Chocolate Cake

Cuisine: American | Category: Dessert | Difficulty: Medium
Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 35 minutes | Servings: 12
Dietary Notes: Contains gluten, dairy, eggs

### Ingredients
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1 cup hot water
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two 9-inch round cake pans.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.
3. Add eggs, buttermilk, vegetable oil, and vanilla extract. Beat with an electric mixer on medium speed for 2 minutes until smooth.
4. Stir in hot water — the batter will be very thin, this is normal.
5. Divide batter evenly between the two prepared pans.
6. Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
7. Cool in pans for 10 minutes, then turn out onto wire racks to cool completely.
8. Frost with your favorite chocolate frosting once completely cooled.

### Chef's Tips
- Using hot coffee instead of hot water intensifies the chocolate flavor without making the cake taste like coffee.
- For a sugar-free version, substitute sugar with monk fruit sweetener or erythritol at a 1:1 ratio.
- For a gluten-free version, use a 1:1 gluten-free flour blend.
- Buttermilk can be substituted with 1 cup milk + 1 tablespoon lemon juice.

### Nutritional Information (per serving, approximate)
Calories: 290 | Protein: 4g | Carbs: 48g | Fat: 11g | Fiber: 2g

## Chicken Tikka Masala

Cuisine: Indian | Category: Main Course | Difficulty: Medium
Prep Time: 20 minutes (plus marinating) | Cook Time: 40 minutes | Servings: 4
Dietary Notes: Contains dairy; gluten-free

### Ingredients
Marinade:
- 1 lb (450g) boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 teaspoons ground turmeric
- 2 teaspoons garam masala
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon red chili powder
- 1 teaspoon salt

Sauce:
- 2 tablespoons butter or ghee
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
- 2 teaspoons garam masala
- 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- Fresh cilantro for garnish
- Salt to taste

### Instructions
1. Combine all marinade ingredients in a bowl. Add chicken pieces and coat well. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, preferably overnight.
2. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Spread marinated chicken on a lined baking sheet. Bake for 15 minutes until lightly charred.
3. In a large deep skillet or Dutch oven, melt butter over medium heat. Add diced onion and sauté for 5-6 minutes until softened and golden.
4. Add garlic and ginger, cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Add garam masala, turmeric, cumin, and paprika. Stir for 30 seconds to toast the spices.
6. Pour in crushed tomatoes. Bring to a simmer and cook for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
7. Reduce heat to low. Stir in heavy cream and sugar. Add the baked chicken pieces.
8. Simmer for 10 minutes, allowing flavors to meld. Season with salt to taste.
9. Garnish with fresh cilantro. Serve hot with basmati rice or warm naan bread.

### Chef's Tips
- For a dairy-free version, use coconut cream instead of heavy cream and coconut yogurt for the marinade.
- For a vegan version, substitute chicken with firm tofu or chickpeas.
- Kashmiri chili powder gives a beautiful color without too much heat.
- Leftovers taste even better the next day as the flavors develop.

### Nutritional Information (per serving, approximate)
Calories: 480 | Protein: 32g | Carbs: 18g | Fat: 32g | Fiber: 3g

## Pasta Aglio e Olio

Cuisine: Italian | Category: Main Course | Difficulty: Easy
Prep Time: 5 minutes | Cook Time: 15 minutes | Servings: 4
Dietary Notes: Vegan (without parmesan); contains gluten

### Ingredients
- 1 lb (450g) spaghetti
- 6 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (adjust to taste)
- 1/4 cup fresh Italian parsley, chopped
- Salt to taste
- Freshly grated Parmesan cheese (optional, omit for vegan)
- 1 cup reserved pasta water

### Instructions
1. Bring a large pot of heavily salted water to a rolling boil. Cook spaghetti according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
2. While pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add sliced garlic and cook slowly for 2-3 minutes, stirring frequently, until garlic is golden and fragrant. Do not burn the garlic.
3. Add red pepper flakes and cook for 30 seconds.
4. Add drained pasta directly to the skillet. Toss to coat with the garlic oil.
5. Add pasta water a few tablespoons at a time, tossing continuously, until a light, silky sauce forms around the pasta.
6. Remove from heat. Add chopped parsley and toss well.
7. Serve immediately with freshly grated Parmesan if desired.

### Chef's Tips
- The key is cooking the garlic LOW and SLOW — golden, never brown or burnt.
- Using bronze-die pasta helps the sauce cling better.
- For added protein, toss in some sautéed shrimp or white beans.
- A squeeze of lemon juice at the end brightens all the flavors.

### Nutritional Information (per serving, approximate)
Calories: 520 | Protein: 14g | Carbs: 72g | Fat: 20g | Fiber: 3g

## Thai Green Curry with Vegetables

Cuisine: Thai | Category: Main Course | Difficulty: Easy
Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 25 minutes | Servings: 4
Dietary Notes: Vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free

### Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons coconut oil
- 3 tablespoons Thai green curry paste
- 1 can (14 oz) full-fat coconut milk
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce (use tamari for gluten-free)
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar or palm sugar
- 1 medium eggplant, cubed
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 cup green beans, trimmed and halved
- 1 cup bamboo shoots
- 1 block (14 oz) firm tofu, cubed
- Juice of 1 lime
- Fresh Thai basil leaves
- Steamed jasmine rice for serving

### Instructions
1. Heat coconut oil in a large wok or deep pan over medium-high heat.
2. Add green curry paste and stir-fry for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Pour in half the coconut milk and cook for 2 minutes, stirring, until the oil separates slightly.
4. Add the remaining coconut milk, vegetable broth, soy sauce, and sugar. Bring to a gentle boil.
5. Add eggplant and cook for 5 minutes until it starts to soften.
6. Add bell pepper, green beans, bamboo shoots, and tofu. Simmer for 10-12 minutes until all vegetables are tender.
7. Remove from heat. Stir in lime juice and tear in fresh Thai basil leaves.
8. Serve over steamed jasmine rice.

### Chef's Tips
- For a spicier curry, add 1-2 fresh bird's eye chilies, sliced.
- You can substitute tofu with chicken, shrimp, or just use more vegetables.
- Mae Ploy or Maesri are excellent curry paste brands.
- Kaffir lime leaves add an authentic aroma — add 3-4 leaves in step 4 if available.

### Nutritional Information (per serving, approximate)
Calories: 380 | Protein: 16g | Carbs: 22g | Fat: 28g | Fiber: 5g

## Classic Guacamole

Cuisine: Mexican | Category: Appetizer/Dip | Difficulty: Easy
Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 0 minutes | Servings: 6
Dietary Notes: Vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free

### Ingredients
- 3 ripe avocados
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup diced white onion
- 3 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped
- 2 roma tomatoes, diced and seeded
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic
- 1 pinch ground cayenne pepper
- 1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and minced (optional)

### Instructions
1. Cut avocados in half, remove pit, and scoop flesh into a mixing bowl.
2. Add lime juice and salt. Mash with a fork to your desired consistency (chunky or smooth).
3. Fold in onion, cilantro, tomatoes, and garlic.
4. Add cayenne pepper and jalapeño if using. Mix gently.
5. Taste and adjust salt and lime juice as needed.
6. Serve immediately with tortilla chips, or cover with plastic wrap pressed directly onto the surface to prevent browning.

### Chef's Tips
- The best avocados for guacamole yield slightly to gentle pressure and have dark skin.
- A little cumin (1/4 teaspoon) adds a warm, earthy flavor.
- For a fruity twist, add diced mango or pomegranate seeds.
- Press plastic wrap directly onto the guacamole surface to prevent oxidation.

### Nutritional Information (per serving, approximate)
Calories: 160 | Protein: 2g | Carbs: 10g | Fat: 14g | Fiber: 7g

## Mushroom Risotto

Cuisine: Italian | Category: Main Course | Difficulty: Medium
Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 35 minutes | Servings: 4
Dietary Notes: Vegetarian, gluten-free; contains dairy

### Ingredients
- 6 cups vegetable broth, warmed
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 1 pound mixed mushrooms (cremini, shiitake, oyster), sliced
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1.5 cups Arborio rice
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
- 2 tablespoons fresh thyme leaves
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped

### Instructions
1. Heat vegetable broth in a saucepan and keep it warm on low heat throughout cooking.
2. In a large, heavy-bottomed pan, heat 2 tablespoons olive oil over medium-high heat. Add mushrooms and cook for 5-7 minutes until golden brown and all moisture has evaporated. Season with salt. Remove and set aside.
3. In the same pan, add remaining olive oil and butter. Sauté onion for 3-4 minutes until translucent. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
4. Add Arborio rice and stir for 1-2 minutes until edges become translucent.
5. Pour in white wine and stir until fully absorbed.
6. Begin adding warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring frequently. Wait until each addition is nearly absorbed before adding the next. This process takes about 18-20 minutes.
7. When rice is creamy and al dente, stir in the cooked mushrooms, Parmesan cheese, and thyme.
8. Season with salt and pepper. Garnish with fresh parsley.
9. Serve immediately — risotto waits for no one!

### Chef's Tips
- Never wash Arborio rice — the starch is what makes risotto creamy.
- Using dried porcini mushrooms (soaked in hot water) adds incredible depth. Use the soaking liquid as part of your broth.
- For a vegan version, skip the butter and Parmesan; use nutritional yeast for umami.
- The finished risotto should flow like lava when plated, not sit in a stiff mound.

### Nutritional Information (per serving, approximate)
Calories: 420 | Protein: 12g | Carbs: 58g | Fat: 16g | Fiber: 3g

## Grilled Salmon with Lemon Dill Sauce

Cuisine: American/Scandinavian | Category: Main Course | Difficulty: Easy
Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 12 minutes | Servings: 4
Dietary Notes: Gluten-free, dairy-free (without butter), high-protein, keto-friendly

### Ingredients
Salmon:
- 4 salmon fillets (6 oz each), skin-on
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper

Lemon Dill Sauce:
- 1/3 cup Greek yogurt (or mayo for dairy-free)
- 2 tablespoons fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon lemon zest
- 1 small clove garlic, minced
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions
1. Pat salmon fillets dry with paper towels. Brush with olive oil and season with garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
2. Preheat grill or grill pan to medium-high heat (about 400°F). Oil the grates well.
3. Place salmon skin-side down on the grill. Cook for 5-6 minutes without moving.
4. Carefully flip and cook for another 3-4 minutes until internal temperature reaches 145°F (63°C).
5. While salmon grills, prepare the sauce: whisk together Greek yogurt, dill, lemon juice, lemon zest, garlic, salt, and pepper.
6. Serve salmon with a generous dollop of lemon dill sauce. Pair with roasted asparagus or a fresh green salad.

### Chef's Tips
- Let the salmon come to room temperature for 15 minutes before grilling for even cooking.
- Don't overcook — salmon continues to cook for a few minutes after removing from heat.
- For an omega-3 boost, choose wild-caught salmon over farm-raised.
- Cedar plank grilling adds a wonderful smoky flavor.

### Nutritional Information (per serving, approximate)
Calories: 350 | Protein: 38g | Carbs: 2g | Fat: 22g | Fiber: 0g

## Banana Bread

Cuisine: American | Category: Baking/Breakfast | Difficulty: Easy
Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 60 minutes | Servings: 10
Dietary Notes: Vegetarian; contains gluten, dairy, eggs

### Ingredients
- 3 very ripe bananas, mashed
- 1/3 cup melted butter
- 3/4 cup sugar (or 1/2 cup honey)
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- Pinch of salt
- 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup chopped walnuts (optional)
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon (optional)

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x5 inch loaf pan.
2. In a large bowl, mash the bananas with a fork until smooth but slightly chunky.
3. Mix in melted butter, sugar, beaten egg, and vanilla extract.
4. Sprinkle baking soda and salt over the mixture and stir to combine.
5. Add flour and fold gently until just combined — do not overmix!
6. Fold in walnuts and cinnamon if using.
7. Pour batter into prepared loaf pan.
8. Bake for 55-65 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. The top should be golden brown and slightly cracked.
9. Let cool in pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack.

### Chef's Tips
- The riper the bananas, the better — look for bananas with lots of brown spots.
- You can freeze overripe bananas and thaw them for baking anytime.
- For a vegan version, use a flax egg (1 tbsp ground flaxseed + 3 tbsp water) and coconut oil instead of butter.
- Add chocolate chips for an indulgent twist.
- Banana bread freezes beautifully — wrap tightly and freeze for up to 3 months.

### Nutritional Information (per serving, approximate)
Calories: 220 | Protein: 3g | Carbs: 38g | Fat: 7g | Fiber: 1g

## Vegetable Biryani

Cuisine: Indian | Category: Main Course | Difficulty: Medium
Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cook Time: 40 minutes | Servings: 6
Dietary Notes: Vegetarian, can be made vegan (substitute ghee with oil, omit yogurt)

### Ingredients
- 2 cups basmati rice, soaked for 30 minutes
- 3 tablespoons ghee or vegetable oil
- 2 bay leaves
- 4 green cardamom pods
- 4 whole cloves
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 1 large onion, thinly sliced
- 2 teaspoons ginger-garlic paste
- 2 green chilies, slit
- 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, beans, potatoes), diced
- 1/2 cup cauliflower florets
- 1/4 cup plain yogurt
- 2 teaspoons biryani masala
- 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 teaspoon red chili powder
- Salt to taste
- 1/4 cup fresh mint leaves
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- Pinch of saffron soaked in 2 tablespoons warm milk
- 3.5 cups water
- Fried onions for garnish

### Instructions
1. Wash and soak basmati rice for 30 minutes. Drain.
2. Heat ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot. Add bay leaves, cardamom, cloves, and cinnamon. Sauté for 30 seconds until aromatic.
3. Add sliced onion and cook until deep golden brown (8-10 minutes). This step is crucial for flavor.
4. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilies. Cook for 1 minute.
5. Add all the vegetables and sauté for 3-4 minutes.
6. Add yogurt, biryani masala, turmeric, chili powder, and salt. Mix well and cook for 2 minutes.
7. Add the drained rice and gently mix to coat with the masala.
8. Pour in water. Add half the mint and cilantro. Bring to a boil.
9. Once boiling, reduce heat to the lowest setting. Cover with a tight-fitting lid (seal with dough if needed). Cook for 20 minutes without opening the lid.
10. Drizzle saffron milk over the top. Sprinkle remaining mint and cilantro. Cover and let rest for 5 minutes.
11. Gently fluff with a fork. Garnish with fried onions and serve with raita.

### Chef's Tips
- The dum cooking method (sealed, low heat) is what makes biryani special — resist the urge to peek!
- Soaking the rice is essential for long, separate grains.
- For a richer biryani, layer the rice and vegetables and cook in the dum style.
- Serve with cucumber raita and mirchi ka salan (chili peanut gravy) for an authentic experience.

### Nutritional Information (per serving, approximate)
Calories: 340 | Protein: 7g | Carbs: 56g | Fat: 10g | Fiber: 4g

## Mediterranean Quinoa Salad

Cuisine: Mediterranean | Category: Salad/Side | Difficulty: Easy
Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 15 minutes | Servings: 6
Dietary Notes: Vegan, gluten-free, high-protein

### Ingredients
- 1.5 cups quinoa, rinsed
- 3 cups water
- 1 English cucumber, diced
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1/2 red onion, finely diced
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 cup kalamata olives, halved
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 1/4 cup fresh mint, chopped

Dressing:
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 3 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions
1. Combine quinoa and water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes until water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and let cool.
2. While quinoa cools, prepare all vegetables and herbs.
3. Whisk together all dressing ingredients in a small bowl.
4. In a large bowl, combine cooled quinoa, cucumber, tomatoes, bell pepper, red onion, chickpeas, olives, parsley, and mint.
5. Pour dressing over the salad and toss gently to combine.
6. Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve at room temperature or chilled.

### Chef's Tips
- This salad tastes even better after a few hours as the flavors marry together.
- Add crumbled feta cheese for a vegetarian (non-vegan) version.
- Toasting the quinoa in a dry pan for 2 minutes before cooking adds a nutty flavor.
- Great for meal prep — keeps well in the fridge for 3-4 days.

### Nutritional Information (per serving, approximate)
Calories: 310 | Protein: 11g | Carbs: 40g | Fat: 13g | Fiber: 7g
